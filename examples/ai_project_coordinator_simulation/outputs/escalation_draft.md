**Schedule impact:**

**Scope impact:**
- Adding Northgate South ED was not included in the original contract/charter and would add discovery/training/support complexity. Formal change control is required.

**Cost / effort impact:**
- **OPEN** (requires impact assessment; likely additional effort/resources if scope expands or timeline compresses)

- **Description:** Re-baseline go-live to a later date aligned to integration sign-off + UAT + training readiness.
- **Pros:** Higher confidence in readiness; reduces risk of rushed launch and instability.
- **Cons / risks:** Misses current 2026-05-01 target; executive expectations reset required.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** Sponsor approval; updated communications and plan.
- **Schedule implication:** Go-live date would shift later (exact date **OPEN** pending re-plan).

### Option B — Phase the rollout (maintain 2026-05-01 for original scope only)
- **Description:** Keep 2026-05-01 for original 3-campus in-scope population (Main/Eastside/Westview) and defer any expansion (Northgate South ED) to a Phase 2 date.
- **Pros:** Controls scope; reduces added complexity; provides path to keep target date if readiness gates can be met.
- **Cons / risks:** Still tight due to integration slip; requires explicit agreement and strict change control.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** CMO agreement; formal change control for Northgate South request; clear success criteria.
- **Schedule implication:** Phase 2 timeline **OPEN**.

### Option C — Maintain 2026-05-01 AND add Northgate South ED (not recommended without additional changes)
- **Description:** Attempt to deliver expanded scope on original date.
- **Pros:** Meets requested timeline/scope on paper.
- **Cons / risks:** High risk of insufficient validation/training; elevated hypercare incidents; quality risk.
- **Prerequisites / dependencies:** Significant additional resources and rapid approvals (**OPEN** whether possible).
- **Schedule implication:** High probability of missed readiness or unplanned slip.
