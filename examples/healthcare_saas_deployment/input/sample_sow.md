# HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

---

## Overview

**Fictional Health Medical Center – Expected Outcomes / Objectives**

- Enable physicians and care teams to return to face-to-face interactions with their patients.
- Enable EMR adoption/utilization by providing clinicians solutions aligned with organization-wide workflows.
- Reduce clinician burnout by improving first-time accuracy of documentation, increasing clinical efficiency, and reducing re-work.
- Achieve complete and accurate documentation to improve clinical quality, patient care outcomes, and appropriate reimbursement.

---

## Scope

- Support for up to six (6) Authorized Users during the Evaluation Period.
- Support of Fictional Ambient technology for all Authorized Users following the Evaluation Period.
- Support of Fictional Ambient Live Chat Clinical Support and Live Chat Help.
- Within five (5) days of General Availability (GA), Fictional Health AI and Fictional Health Medical Center will establish a mutually agreed project schedule including timeline, dependencies, resource requirements, and milestones.
